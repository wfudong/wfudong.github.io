Practice 7: Review and Integration Bridge

Estimated time: 40-50 minutes

Focus: Higher Math Lecture 7

Practice Mode

Work through the questions, open hints only when needed, then submit to check your score. Numeric answers accept decimals and expressions such as sqrt(2), pi, and exp(1).

  1. Practice 7 · 1

    For $A=(1,0,2)$, $B=(3,-1,1)$, $C=(0,2,4)$, the plane through them is:

    Hint

    Use $AB\times AC=(0,-3,3)$.

    Solution

    The simplified plane is $z-y=2$.

  2. Practice 7 · 2

    The area of triangle $ABC$ above is:

    Hint

    Triangle area is half of $\|AB\times AC\|$. Enter a decimal or $3*sqrt(2)/2$.

    Solution

    The area is $3\sqrt2/2$.

  3. Practice 7 · 3

    If two paths give different values for a multivariable limit, then the limit:

    Hint

    A true limit must be independent of path.

    Solution

    The limit does not exist.

  4. Practice 7 · 4

    For $f=x^2+y^2$ at $(1,2)$ in direction $(0,1)$, the directional derivative is:

    Hint

    Compute $\nabla f=(2x,2y)$ and dot with $(0,1)$.

    Solution

    The value is $4$.

  5. Practice 7 · 5

    Compute $\int_0^2\int_0^1 (x+y)\,dy\,dx$.

    Hint

    Integrate in $y$ first.

    Solution

    The inner integral is $x+1/2$, and the final value is $3$.

  6. Practice 7 · 6

    The area of the triangle $x\ge0$, $y\ge0$, $x+y\le1$ is:

    Hint

    It is a right triangle with legs 1 and 1.

    Solution

    The area is $1/2$.
