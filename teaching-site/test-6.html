Practice 6: Taylor, Inverse, Implicit, and Extrema Review

Estimated time: 35-45 minutes

Focus: Higher Math Lecture 6

Practice Mode

Work through the questions, open hints only when needed, then submit to check your score. Numeric answers accept decimals and expressions such as sqrt(2), pi, and exp(1).

  1. Practice 6 · 1

    For $F(x,y)=(x+y,x-y)$, compute $\det J_F$.

    Hint

    The Jacobian matrix is $\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}$.

    Solution

    $\det J_F=-2$.

  2. Practice 6 · 2

    If $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-1=0$, then $y'(x)$ at $(3/5,4/5)$ is:

    Hint

    Use $y'=-F_x/F_y=-x/y$.

    Solution

    The value is $-(3/5)/(4/5)=-3/4$.

  3. Practice 6 · 3

    For $f(x,y)=e^x\cos y$, the coefficient of $x^2$ in the second-order Taylor polynomial at $(0,0)$ is:

    Hint

    The one-variable $e^x$ part contributes $x^2/2$.

    Solution

    The coefficient is $1/2$.

  4. Practice 6 · 4

    For $f=x^3-3xy+y^3$, compute $D=f_{xx}f_{yy}-f_{xy}^2$ at $(1,1)$.

    Hint

    Here $f_{xx}=6x$, $f_{yy}=6y$, $f_{xy}=-3$.

    Solution

    At $(1,1)$, $D=6\cdot6-9=27$.

  5. Practice 6 · 5

    Which condition lets $F(x,y)=0$ define $y$ locally as a function of $x$?

    Hint

    Solve for the variable whose partial derivative is nonzero.

    Solution

    The condition is $F_y\ne0$.

  6. Practice 6 · 6

    The maximum value of $xy$ on $x^2+y^2=1$ is:

    Hint

    Use $2xy\le x^2+y^2$.

    Solution

    The maximum is $1/2$, reached at $x=y=1/\sqrt2$ or both negative.
