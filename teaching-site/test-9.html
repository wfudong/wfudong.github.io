Practice 9: Integration Theory

Estimated time: 45-60 minutes

Focus: Higher Math Lecture 9

Practice Mode

Work through the questions, open hints only when needed, then submit to check your score. Numeric answers accept decimals and expressions such as sqrt(2), pi, and exp(1).

  1. Practice 9 · 1

    Compute $\iint_{[0,2]\times[1,3]}(x^2+y)\,dA$.

    Hint

    Lecture 9 computes this rectangle example. Enter a decimal or $40/3$.

    Solution

    The value is $40/3$.

  2. Practice 9 · 2

    For the triangle $0\le x$, $0\le y$, $x+y\le1$, compute $\iint_D(x+y)\,dA$.

    Hint

    Use $0\le x\le1$, $0\le y\le1-x$.

    Solution

    The value is $1/3$.

  3. Practice 9 · 3

    The region for $\int_0^1\int_y^1 f(x,y)\,dx\,dy$ is:

    Hint

    Read the inner bounds as $y\le x\le1$.

    Solution

    The region is $0\le y\le x\le1$.

  4. Practice 9 · 4

    Compute the area of the unit disk using polar coordinates.

    Hint

    Use $0\le r\le1$, $0\le\theta\le2\pi$. Enter $pi$ or a decimal.

    Solution

    The value is $\pi$.

  5. Practice 9 · 5

    The volume of the unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is:

    Hint

    Use spherical coordinates. Enter a decimal or $4*pi/3$.

    Solution

    The volume is $4\pi/3$.

  6. Practice 9 · 6

    In cylindrical coordinates, the volume element is:

    Hint

    Cylindrical coordinates are polar in the $xy$-plane plus $z$.

    Solution

    $dV=r\,dr\,d\theta\,dz$.
